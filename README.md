*Controlled and Dynamic Life-Environment Virtualization*
---
**Project CoDLEV** is an ambitious, lifelong personal project centered around **Artificial Life**. The primary purpose of the project is to strengthen my knowledge on data and biology to act as part of my spine for future careers.

At its very essence, the project is recreational. It is not being written in dependence of larger and/or more serious works of greater significance. CoDLEV is purely for **personal studies/research and pastime**.

Organs of CoDLEV
---
CoDLEV is a scalable conglomerate of many components (well, as things should be), primarily made in **Python and Julia**. Here is a couple of its shiny legs.

- Concise and cleanly designed independent variables such as:
	- Tick system
	-  Environment
		- External events
		- Resources
	- Organisms
		- Consumption
		- Reproduction
		- etc.
- Randomness and more to be developed
